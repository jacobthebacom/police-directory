What happens next

We will review your submission against our methodology. If the report can be verified against public records, court documents, or news coverage, it may be added to the directory. If we need clarification, we will reach out to the email address you provided — if you provided one.

A note on anonymity

Per our privacy policy, any correspondence between an individual and USA Cop Watch is kept private and is never shared with third parties.

If your report concerns an emergency

USA Cop Watch is not an emergency service. If you are in danger or reporting an active incident, contact local emergency services or a licensed attorney in your jurisdiction.
