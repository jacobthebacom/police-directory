About USA Cop Watch

USA Cop Watch is an autonomous organization that monitors and publishes police activity across the United States. Our goal is to create a public database that informs residents about the individuals who police them.

USA Cop Watch does not track every new hire across the country and instead focuses primarily on cases of misconduct, lack of accountability, and wandering officers.

Each piece of media that comes to our organization is verified and documented by a human being. However, as we are an unfunded and crowdsourced organization, we do unfortunately utilize AI in our web development.

Methodology

All sources gathered are cross-verified with public information and public media. No source is posted on this website without a human being checking its validity.

Who is not included

Not every officer who has received a complaint is listed here. This database only includes individuals whose records have been verified by USA Cop Watch. The absence of an officer from this database does not imply anything about their conduct, career, or disciplinary history.

Removal & correction policy

Anyone may contest a source or piece of media by emailing USACopWatch@proton.me. All complaints will be thoroughly investigated and responded to. Where the record does not support an entry, we will update or remove it.

Legal disclaimer

No claim is made that any individual committed a crime. All entries link to primary sources, and no entry should be read as an allegation of criminal conduct unless a criminal conviction is explicitly cited.

Privacy policy

The information of anyone in contact with USA Cop Watch is not shared anywhere. Any correspondence between an individual (not an organization) and USA Cop Watch will be kept private.

Data license

The information and sourcing on this site is free to be disseminated in any capacity.

Contact

Right of reply, corrections, and removal requests: USACopWatch@proton.me
